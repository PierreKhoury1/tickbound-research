vast 55936408 (A100-SXM4-40GB, driver 580, nvidia/cuda:12.6.3-devel; ~$0.15; destroyed). Follows docs/sliding_probe.md.

METHOD (tools/instrument.py --probe-after k, tools/make_probes.py, tools/probe_analysis.py)
- One trace build per probe position: the 5 region stamps as before (outside the hot region) plus ONE
  `CS2R R[K+28], SR_CLOCKLO` right after pass-2 instruction k (stall 1, no scoreboard, yield as k; never between an
  operand-reuse pair: the probe moves past k when k carries .reuse bits). Its clock64 is stored by the exit stamp at
  record + 96. Register count K + 34. check_trace_sass.py verifies each build (kernel instructions bit-identical, exactly
  one CS2R inside pass 2 where the sidecar says). Harness writes it as a 6th event "probe".
- probe clock - bar2 clock = issue time of instruction k after pass 2 starts (ISSUE time, not completion: a load's
  arrival shows at its consumer's issue). Median over 64 warps (16 CTAs x 4 warps, 1 CTA per SM) and 5 launches.
- Sweep: N4096_M16_warm, 172 positions x 4 variants (orig/ro/ro2/ro3) in 60 s; N8192_M16_decode, 340 positions.
