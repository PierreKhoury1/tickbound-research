Where can a trace stamp go without changing the bag loops? (NVRTC 12.6.85 PTX + ptxas 12.6.85, sm_80, earlier 82-slot layout)
Each experiment injects a subset of stamps into the PTX of the trace build and compares the hot (bag) loops of every
variant with the timing build (check_trace_sass.py: opcode+operands text, branch targets loop-relative; encodings = both
64-bit words incl. control bits). Tags: E = entry stamp at the top of the kernel; X = exit stamp after the chunk loop;
0..4 = per-chunk stamps at chunk_start / validation_start / main_start / main_end / chunk_end (X2-X4: other exit-stamp forms).
Result: only E keeps every loop byte-identical; any stamp inside or after the chunk loop makes ptxas re-allocate registers
and re-schedule the bag loops. Hence the trace build = entry stamp in source + exit stamp patched into the binary
(tools/patch_exits.py), which keeps all loops byte-identical (sass/check_trace_*.txt).
